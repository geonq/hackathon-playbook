# Safe and Responsible Prototyping

Hackathons reward speed, but a fast prototype can expose data or mislead users. Keep these checks close to the build.

- Use synthetic or explicitly approved data. Do not copy personal, patient, employee, customer, or confidential records into an AI service without permission and a clear basis.
- Keep credentials out of source files, screenshots, transcripts, browser storage shared with the team, and public submissions. Use environment variables and rotate any exposed key.
- Make the prototype's limits visible. Label generated output and provide citations when retrieval is part of the result.
- Keep a person responsible for consequential decisions, especially in health, employment, legal, safety, or public-service workflows.
- Ask before recording people, uploading partner materials, or publishing code, data, names, or logos.
- Confirm ownership, license, open-source, and post-event handoff rules with the organizer and problem owner. Do not infer them from a previous event in the series.
- Do not claim impact, precision, time savings, or acceptance unless you measured it and can explain the test.

For event-specific rules, the organizer's current instructions control. See each event's [source ledger](../events/claude-code-impact-lab-berlin-2026/sources.md) and [Nebius source ledger](../events/nebius-build-week-berlin-2026/sources.md).
